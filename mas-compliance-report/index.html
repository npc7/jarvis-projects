Assessment Overview

This quarterly compliance report provides a comprehensive assessment of TrustIn's adherence to MAS regulatory requirements for Digital Payment Token (DPT) services under the Payment Services Act 2019. The assessment covers Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Counter-Financing of Terrorism (CFT), and sanctions compliance programs.

✓ Key Achievements

  • Full implementation of Travel Rule requirements
  • Real-time transaction monitoring system upgrade
  • Enhanced sanctions screening with daily updates
  • Zero regulatory breaches in reporting period

⚠ Areas for Improvement

  • • Enhanced due diligence documentation gaps
  • • Cross-chain tracking capabilities
  • • Staff training completion rate (87%)
⚖️
Legal Basis
Payment Services Act 2019, Section 28: A licensee shall implement adequate policies, procedures and controls to identify, assess and mitigate money laundering and terrorism financing risks.

PSA 2019 § 28 MAS Notice PSN02 § 6 PS-G01 § 4.2
